# Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics System

## Project Overview

The Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics System is an end-to-end business intelligence and data analytics project designed to transform structured business transaction data into meaningful business insights.

The project covers data preparation, relational database development, SQL analysis, customer analytics, Power BI data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboard development, validation, and business decision support.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to provide management with a centralized analytical solution to understand:

- Overall business performance
- Customer behavior and value
- Product performance and profitability
- Regional performance
- Time-based trends and growth
- Business opportunities and areas requiring attention

- `dim_customer`
- `dim_product`
- `dim_geography`
- `dim_date`
- `fact_transaction`

## Validation

The project includes validation across Excel, SQL, and Power BI.

Validation covered:

- Row counts
- Duplicate primary keys
- Missing values
- Referential integrity
- Financial calculations
- Date range
- SQL vs Power BI reconciliation
- Dashboard KPI accuracy
- Slicer and interaction testing
- Refresh testing

Final validation confirmed that the SQL and Power BI results reconcile with the validated dataset.
